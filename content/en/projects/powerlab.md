A Python library for power electronics and electrical system simulation.

- **Electrical Models**: Battery cells, ideal sources, resistors, RC loads
- **DC-DC Converters**: Buck, Boost, and Buck-Boost converters
- **Controllers**: P, PI, PD, and PID controllers
- **Simulation Tools**: Time-domain simulation with variable tracking
- **Data I/O**: CSV and TXT input/output for simulation data
- **Visualization**: Built-in plotting and system diagram generation
